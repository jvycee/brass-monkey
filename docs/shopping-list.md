## Notes

**Why Kasa/TP-Link:**
- Local API (works without internet)
- No subscription fees
- Excellent Home Assistant integration
- Reliable hardware

**Why Aqara:**
- Good quality sensors
- Small, unobtrusive
- Long battery life (1-2 years)
- Zigbee = local control

**Avoid:**
- Tuya/Smart Life devices (cloud dependent)
- Ring/Amazon devices (privacy concerns)
- Cheap no-name brands (unreliable)
